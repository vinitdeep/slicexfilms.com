## Brand & Style

The brand identity targets ultra-luxury wedding cinematographers, couture wedding planners, and high-net-worth couples seeking heirloom visual storytelling. The design evokes the visceral atmosphere of a private screening room in an haute couture fashion house: intimate, cinematic, impeccably curated, and profoundly emotional.

Drawing inspiration from editorial luxury minimalism, film grain warmth, and chiaroscuro lighting, the aesthetic relies on deep obsidian depths contrasted against liquid champagne golds and warm amber luminance. Every surface feels deliberate, weighted, and bespoke, eliminating digital sterility in favor of analog warmth and filmic gravitas.

## Colors

The palette embraces pure dark-mode immersion, mirroring the dark velvet interior of a luxury theater:

- **Primary (`#D4AF37`)**: Refined warm champagne gold for definitive actions, active states, key focus borders, and primary accolades.
- **Secondary (`#C88A42`)**: Luminous warm bronze/amber for radiant glows, playback timelines, and secondary focal accents.
- **Tertiary (`#9B4A40`)**: Rich terracotta-rose with velvety undertones for delicate tags, emotional cues, badges, and warm highlight shifts.
- **Neutral Base (`#0A0A0A`)**: Deep obsidian base surface, tiered with `#121212` for elevated cards, `#181816` for modals, and `#FBFBF9` for pristine ivory typography.
- **Muted Gray-Champagne (`#2E2B25`)**: Used exclusively for hairlines, structural dividers, and ghost borders to preserve subtle visual containment without harsh contrast.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial tension between the romantic drama of haute couture and the precision of digital film production. 

- **Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`)**: Employs classical transitional proportions, graceful flourishes, and high stroke contrast. All large display treatments evoke timeless film title sequences and high-fashion editorial mastheads.
- **Body & Labels (`Manrope`)**: Provides technical clarity and crisp contrast against black backgrounds. At smaller scales, body copy remains ultra-legible with open apertures and neutral proportions, while labels use expanded tracking to evoke gallery plaques and production slate tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column fluid grid system on desktop (4-column on mobile, 8-column on tablet) framed with cinematic letterbox proportions. Media viewports utilize widescreen aspect ratios (2.39:1 anamorphic or 16:9) flanked by generous, asymmetric margins that let still frames breathe.

Breakpoints transition at:
- **Mobile**: `< 768px` (compact single-column vertical film rolls with 1.25rem safe margins)
- **Tablet**: `768px - 1024px` (balanced 8-column presentation with 2rem margins)
- **Desktop**: `> 1024px` (expansive 12-column staggered editorial grid with 3rem margins and 1440px max-width containment)

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons traditional drop shadows in favor of ambient filmic luminescence and low-contrast borders:

- **Surface Tiers**: Canvas ground rests at `#0A0A0A`. Cards and media wells lift to `#121212`. Hover states and active layers settle on `#1A1916`.
- **Low-Contrast Hairlines**: Crisp 1px borders in `#2E2B25` or translucent `#D4AF3726` replace physical elevation, outlining panels like precision-cut film mounts.
- **Atmospheric Amber Glows**: Floating elements, modal backdrops, and active video players cast an extra-diffused radial halo (`0 0 60px -10px rgba(200, 138, 66, 0.12)` combined with `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`).
- **Glassmorphic Screening Surfaces**: Overlays and timeline scrubbers utilize `rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.75)` with `16px` backdrop-filter blur, preserving color temperature beneath.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined, architectural soft shape language (`roundedness: 1`). Elements have sharp, precise corners tempered by subtle 4px (`0.25rem`) radii on buttons and input fields, expanding to 8px (`0.5rem`) on media cards and preview sheets. This avoids casual roundness, preserving the crisp discipline of photographic framing and fine gallery mounts.

## Components

- **Buttons**:
  - *Primary*: Champagne gold gradient fill (`linear-gradient(135deg, #E5C378 0%, #D4AF37 50%, #C5A059 100%)`) with rich midnight text (`#0A0A0A`), tracked label typography, and subtle golden bloom on hover.
  - *Secondary / Ghost*: `#121212` background bordered by a 1px `#2E2B25` hairline; transitions to `#D4AF37` hairline with warm ivory text (`#FBFBF9`).
- **Cards (Reels & Film Collections)**:
  - Deep obsidian background with a faint 1px inner border. Images and video stills sit full-bleed or framed with 8px internal padding. On hover, the inner border shifts to luminous amber (`#C88A4250`) accompanied by an atmospheric amber back-glow.
- **Chips & Badges**:
  - Compact height, uppercase tracked `label-sm` text. Terracotta-rose tint (`background: rgba(155, 74, 64, 0.15); border: 1px solid rgba(155, 74, 64, 0.35); color: #E5A49D;`) for film grade tags, 4K/HDR metadata, and couple monogram markers.
- **Input Fields**:
  - Understated surface (`#141412`), 1px `#2E2B25` outline, warm ivory text, and muted golden placeholder text. On focus, the border illuminates to solid champagne gold (`#D4AF37`) with a 2px diffuse glow (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios**:
  - Geometric boxes with subtle 2px rounding or precise circles. Unchecked: 1px `#2E2B25` border over `#121212`. Checked: Solid champagne gold fill featuring a rich obsidian check icon.
- **Film Scrubber & Timeline Player (Product-Specific)**:
  - Ultra-thin 2px track in muted bronze-gray, with active playhead progress rendered in glowing amber (`#C88A42`). Diamond-cut champagne gold playhead thumb that expands on hover.
